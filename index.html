About

I am a chemical engineering graduate from the University of Rajshahi. I work as a Research Assistant in the Polymer and Textile Research Laboratory, where I make and test polymer-based materials for environmental use.

My interests are polymer science, nanomaterials, sustainable materials and advanced polymer composites. I like the full cycle of materials work: synthesis, characterization and performance testing.

My long-term goal is to do advanced research in materials science and build sustainable solutions to environmental and technological problems. I want to grow as a researcher through collaborative, interdisciplinary projects.

Research

In progress

Antibacterial polymer films for water filtration

Polymer and Textile Research Laboratory, University of Rajshahi

I am developing antibacterial films for potential use in water filtration. The work covers PVA/chitosan-based composite membranes with nano-Ni and carbon-based materials, and bio-based chitosan membranes loaded with neem oil. The aim is a stable film that interacts well with water and resists bacteria.

  • Optimizing composition and conditions to improve membrane stability, water interaction and function.
  • Linking structure to properties through characterization and performance tests.
  1. MakePolymer solutions and solution-cast composite films
  2. CharacterizeFTIR, XRD, FESEM, UV-Vis, TGA
  3. TestSwelling, water solubility, contact angle, antibacterial and photocatalytic activity

Carbon capture technologies review

Project supervised by Md. Hasinur Rahman, Lecturer, Department of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering

A comprehensive review of recent advances in major carbon capture technologies. It grew into conference work on choosing an optimum adsorbent for effective decarbonization.
